# Glossary

**Affordability** — Whether a hypothetical purchase can be made without breaching the user's preferred cash buffer, given current balance and projected near-term obligations.

**Burn rate** — See Monthly Burn.

**Cash buffer** — The number of days a user's current liquid balance would last at their average daily expense rate; a core early-warning metric.

**Cash flow** — The movement of money in and out of a user's accounts over a period; distinct from balance, which is a point-in-time snapshot.

**Confidence** — A qualitative (Low/Medium/High) or quantitative (0–1) measure of how much a prediction or recommendation should be trusted, based on data completeness, freshness, historical consistency, and forecast uncertainty.

**Debt-to-income (DTI)** — Total monthly debt payments divided by total monthly income, expressed as a percentage; a standard measure of debt burden.

**Debt service ratio** — Similar to DTI but computed against take-home (net) income where available, rather than gross declared income.

**Discretionary spending** — Spending that is optional/lifestyle-driven (dining, entertainment, shopping) as opposed to fixed (rent, EMI) or variable-but-necessary (groceries, utilities) spending.

**Fixed expense** — A recurring obligation of a largely constant amount (rent, loan EMI, insurance premium).

**Forecast** — A projection of future financial state (typically balance) derived from historical patterns and known scheduled flows, always accompanied by a confidence level.

**Impact simulation** — The "what if I do this?" feature: a side-by-side comparison of a baseline projection and a proposed-scenario projection resulting from a hypothetical financial action.

**Recommendation** — A specific, quantified suggested action generated from an active risk or opportunity, always paired with an expected impact and confidence.

**Recurring expense** — A payment obligation that repeats at a regular interval, detected from ≥2 observed cycles or explicitly declared by the user.

**Risk event** — A detected condition (e.g., low cash buffer, upcoming cash-flow gap, debt pressure) that may negatively affect the user's financial health, carrying severity, evidence, and confidence.

**Savings rate** — Savings (income minus expenses) divided by income, expressed as a percentage; a core measure of financial discipline.

**Variable expense** — A necessary but fluctuating cost (groceries, utilities) as distinguished from fixed or discretionary spending.
